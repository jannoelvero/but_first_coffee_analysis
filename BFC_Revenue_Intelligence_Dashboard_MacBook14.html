Evidence & Methodology

This dashboard preserves the analytical limits established in the Jupyter/Tableau workflow.

  • Pricing: five directly matched beverage families reduce product-mix confounding. BFC had the lowest representative price in all five.
  • Inference: Friedman Q(2)=10.00, p=.0067; Kendall’s W=1.00 indicates complete rank consistency across the five matched families. Pairwise exact Wilcoxon tests were not significant after Bonferroni adjustment.
  • Promotions: 45 of 50 observed BFC menu observations carried a 20% promotional price in the collected snapshot. This is not evidence of a permanent promotion policy.
  • Customer experience: 104 BFC reviews are external descriptive evidence; prevalence means the percentage mentioning a dimension, not dissatisfaction.
  • Profitability: no actual contribution margin, ROI, or profit is estimated without POS, product-cost, promotion-history and channel economics data.
